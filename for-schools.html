What we provide

Three ways schools use us

Small group intervention

Targeted groups for a cohort that needs the same thing, taught live as a small group.

  • Grouped by need, not by class
  • Recorded and written up per session

One-to-one catch-up

For pupils who need individual attention, including those you are supporting through tutoring funding.

  • Matched to subject and stage
  • Attendance and notes on record

Specialist cover

Where a subject is hard to staff, particularly the sciences and A-Level Maths.

  • Taught to your exam board
  • Continuity across a term or a year
How it works

From first call to first lesson

01

A conversation about the cohort

Which pupils, which subject, which exam board, and what you need to be able to show at the end of it.

02

A written proposal

Group sizes, timetable, tutor and cost, quoted per programme rather than per hour off a rate card.

03

Safeguarding and paperwork

DBS certificates, our safeguarding policy and data agreement, in place before anyone teaches.

04

Teaching, with a record

Sessions run to timetable. Attendance, notes and recordings are available to the school throughout.
