The teaching process

How a lesson is taught,
step by step

The same six steps run every week, in the same order. Select any step to see what happens in it.

Before the lesson

The tutor reads last week's report

Every session is written up, so the tutor starts by reading what happened last time: what was covered, what the student found hard, and what was set as homework. The lesson is planned from that, not from a syllabus running to its own schedule.

Report in the portal, family view
In the lesson

Taught live, on a shared whiteboard

Lessons are taught live in a small group on our own platform. The tutor works on a whiteboard the student can write on too, so it is a lesson rather than a broadcast, and the whole board is saved when the lesson ends.

Whiteboard shot, or a photograph of a lesson
At the end of the lesson

Homework is set on what was just taught

Practice goes out on exactly what the lesson covered, while it is still fresh. It is specific rather than a chapter of a book, so the student knows what they are meant to be getting better at.

No screenshot needed for this step
Straight afterwards

The tutor writes the report

What was covered, what went well, what needs work. It goes into the portal the same day, where the student and the parent can both read it. This is the record the next lesson is planned from, which is why it is written while the lesson is fresh.

A written report, family view
During the week

The student works on it in their own time

The recording, the saved whiteboard and the homework are all in the portal, so the lesson can be gone over again as many times as it takes. The report says what to spend that time on.

The portal, family view: a recording and a saved whiteboard
The following week

It starts again from the report

The tutor opens the note from last time and begins there. Nothing is repeated because it was already secure, and nothing is quietly dropped because everyone forgot it was a problem.

No screenshot needed for this step
