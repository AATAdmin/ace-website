Questions, answered

Everything you
might be wondering

It’s a relaxed 15-minute video call where we get to know your child’s needs, answer your questions, and introduce a tutor we think is the right fit. There’s no cost, no card required, and no obligation to continue.

We match on subject, exam board, level and personality. Every tutor is university-educated, DBS-checked and interviewed in person, only the strongest applicants join the team. If a match isn’t perfect, we’ll happily introduce someone else.

One-to-one lessons are £35/hour for a single weekly session, and the hourly rate drops the more sessions you book each week, down to £28/hour at five sessions a week (20% off). Pay monthly upfront to save a further 5%. There are no hidden fees, and everything starts with a free consultation.

Lessons take place in our purpose-built online classroom, live video and audio, a shared interactive whiteboard, screen-sharing and PDFs. It’s a real lesson, not just a video call. You’ll receive a short progress note after every session so you always know how your child is getting on.
