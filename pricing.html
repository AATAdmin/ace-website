    Why does the hourly rate change with the number of sessions?

    The more sessions a week, the lower the rate per hour, and five sessions a week is the lowest we go. Paying monthly upfront takes a further 5% off whichever rate you land on. Set the options above and the figure updates.

    Do we pay for the trial lesson?

    No. The trial lesson is free. One full hour with a tutor, and we do not take a card to book it. You get the written report afterwards, the same as any other lesson, and there is no obligation to carry on.

    What happens if we need to cancel a lesson?

    Cancel or move it with 24 hours’ notice and there is nothing to pay. Rescheduling is free.

    Are there any other fees?

    No joining fee, no registration charge and no materials cost. The rate shown above is the price.

    How do we pay?

    Monthly. Invoices and what you have paid sit in your login, alongside the schedule and the lesson reports, so it is all in one place rather than spread across messages.

    Can we change tutor if it is not working?

    For one-to-one we choose the tutor rather than assigning whoever is free, on the subject, the level, and how your child actually learns. Tutors are university-educated with degree-level knowledge of the subject they teach, and hold an enhanced DBS before a first lesson. We can produce the paperwork if you ask.

    If the tutor turns out not to be the right fit, ask for a different one. It is free, you do not need to give a reason, and nothing else about your booking changes.

    Need help deciding on a plan?

    You should not have to guess how much tutoring your child needs. Tell us the year group and what they are finding hard, and we will tell you what we would do.
