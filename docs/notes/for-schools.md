# Design notes: `for-schools.html`

Moved out of the page source so they are not visible in view-source on the
live site. These are the decisions for this page: what was deliberately left
out and why, what must not change, and which instructions conflicted.

**Read this before editing the page.**

## Block 1

```
============================================================
  NOTE FOR CLAUDE CODE, schools
  ============================================================
  B2B page. It deliberately does NOT route to the free trial form,
  which is built for parents: schools contact us directly, and the
  CTA is email and phone.

  NO CASE STUDIES, no named schools, no pupil-progress figures and
  no "worked with N schools" claims. None have been supplied with
  permission, and inventing them is not an option. When a real
  school agrees to be named, that is a section of its own.

  Nothing here commits to a price. School work is quoted per
  programme, and the site must not imply the parent rate applies.

  Do not reinstate: the free written assessment, the 15-minute
  consultation, "matched within 48 hours", or the gold accent.
  ============================================================
```
