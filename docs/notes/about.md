# Design notes: `about.html`

Moved out of the page source so they are not visible in view-source on the
live site. These are the decisions for this page: what was deliberately left
out and why, what must not change, and which instructions conflicted.

**Read this before editing the page.**

## Block 1

```
============================================================
     ABOUT.

     WRITTEN AS AN ORGANISATION, NOT AS PEOPLE. "We" throughout,
     never "I". The page must still read correctly when the company
     has twenty tutors and staff the founders have never met, so
     nothing on it depends on a specific person. NO founder
     biographies, NO photographs of individuals, NO "meet the team".
     A name may appear only as one clause of company history, which
     is exactly what section 2 does and no more.

     THIS REVERSES AN EARLIER INSTRUCTION, deliberately, on the
     later brief's authority. The previous version had two founder
     cards with photographs, credentials and "Dr Hamza Liaquet" with
     his three degrees listed, which the founder had asked for
     directly. All of it is gone. The doctorate now survives as one
     clause of company history. assets/cofounder.png is no longer
     referenced by this page; the file is left in place because
     become-a-tutor and any future page may want it. If the founders
     want the cards back, they were in the previous version and can
     be restored from the chat record: this was a brief decision,
     not an oversight.

     REGISTER. The page describes how Ace works and stops. It does
     NOT argue we are good, does NOT compare us to agencies,
     marketplaces, school or "traditional tutoring", and does NOT
     tell the reader what to conclude. There is no "why choose us".
     Understate; shortest true sentence wins. Explain anything
     technical by WHAT A PARENT SEES, never by how it works inside,
     and give no rationale. Banned: passionate, excellence, bespoke,
     cutting-edge, revolutionise, transform, unlock, empower,
     journey, industry-leading. No exclamation marks, no rhetorical
     questions, no em-dashes.

     NO SCALE, IN EITHER DIRECTION. No student numbers, no "hundreds
     of families", and equally no offices, departments, regional
     coverage, headcount or "nationwide". Sounding established must
     not become claiming a size we do not have. Also: no
     testimonials, quotes, ratings or review counts, not even
     placeholders. No results or grade claims. No tutor headshots or
     named tutors. No awards, accreditations, memberships,
     partnerships or press. Exactly ONE interactive lesson is
     finished and the page describes that one. Marking is ASSISTED
     and anything uncertain goes to a person: never "instant AI
     marking". Present tense only for what exists, no dates.

     TEACHER LANGUAGE, NOT PRODUCT LANGUAGE. The founders read an
     earlier version of the progress section as corporate jargon,
     and they were right: it said "skills", "statements", "subject
     content", "the system", "before it counts". It now opens on a
     worked example a parent recognises (algebra is not one thing;
     expanding brackets, factorising, quadratics, completing the
     square) and the four bands are described the way a teacher
     describes them to a parent: "comes unstuck when they get
     harder", "still does it weeks later". Keep it in that register.
     Do not reintroduce "skills mapped to statements", DfE statement
     counts, or anything that reads like a product spec.

     SECTION 4 states WHAT IS IN the report and what a parent can
     actually do with it, because "a written report" on its own told
     them nothing they had not assumed. Attendance is deliberately
     no longer its own card: parents supervising a lesson already
     know whether their child turned up, so it sits as one clause
     inside the lessons-and-payments card instead.

     "BETWEEN THE LESSONS" exists because the founders pointed out
     that the page read as though learning only happens in the
     tutored hour. It does not: homework is set on the lesson, a
     student can practise any topic in their subject on their own,
     the tutor sees what they did, and that self-directed work moves
     the same topic picture. Keep the last point; it is the whole
     reason the section is there. Do NOT add interactive lessons to
     this section, they live in "Free things anyone can use", and do
     NOT imply a library of self-study material: exactly one
     interactive lesson is finished.

     SECTION 7, "If it is not working", is the unusual one and the
     reason to keep it: it gives a parent the actual route to change
     tutor and says plainly that doing so is normal and free. Most
     tutoring sites have nothing like it. Do not soften it into a
     general contact block.

     THREE CONFLICTS WITH WHAT THE FOUNDERS SAID DIRECTLY. Their
     answers were kept. The first two are now on their fourth round
     and want settling once, at source:
     a) "Free 15-minute consultation". Retired across all 17 pages
        and replaced with a free trial lesson, one full hour, no
        card, no obligation. The trial is what this page offers.
        Reinstating the consultation here alone would contradict
        every other page.
     b) "The only live route is phone, do not link a contact page".
        get-started.html is live and is the single enquiry route the
        other 16 pages point at, with a fail-loud fallback to phone
        and email. It is the close here, quietly, with phone and
        email beside it. No link to contact.html.
     c) "No group or classroom teaching described as running." The
        founders stated directly, one message before this brief
        arrived, that small groups ARE taught. The opening therefore
        says "one to one and in small groups". pricing.html owns the
        class detail; do not add a schedule, group size or track
        record here.
     ============================================================
```
