# Design notes: `portal.html`

Moved out of the page source so they are not visible in view-source on the
live site. These are the decisions for this page: what was deliberately left
out and why, what must not change, and which instructions conflicted.

**Read this before editing the page.**

## Block 1

```
============================================================
  NOTE FOR CLAUDE CODE, the portal explainer
  ============================================================
  This page EXPLAINS the portal. It is not the portal. The portal
  itself is a separate app on Vercel at
  https://portal.aceacademictutors.com, cross-origin, with its own
  sign-in. Keep that distinction in nav labels and copy.

  NO SCREENSHOTS on this page. The images that used to be here were
  ADMIN screens showing bank balance, margin and named students,
  which is why they were deleted. If a screenshot is ever added it
  must be the family view with dummy data only.

  Do not reinstate: the free written assessment, the 15-minute
  consultation, "matched within 48 hours", or the gold accent.
  ============================================================
```
