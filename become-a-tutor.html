We are a small company and we teach the lessons ourselves. Tutors teach their own subject, keep their own students, and are trusted to plan the teaching rather than deliver someone else's worksheet.

Live online, from homeMaths, English, Biology, Chemistry, Physics
What we look for

Three things, in this order

Real command of the subject

A degree in what you teach, or a strong reason we should think otherwise. You will teach one or two subjects, not whatever is going.

You can explain it

Knowing a subject and teaching it are different skills. The interview includes teaching something to us.

Safeguarding, without exception

Enhanced DBS check before you teach a single lesson. Lessons are recorded, which protects you as much as the student.

How it works

From application to first lesson

01

Send us your CV

Email it with the subjects and levels you teach, and the exam boards you know well.

02

An interview, with teaching in it

A conversation about your subject, then you teach us something. We are looking at how you explain, not how you present.

03

Checks and onboarding

Enhanced DBS check, references, and time on the platform so the first lesson is not the first time you have used it. Rates are agreed at this stage.

04

Your first students

Matched to your subject and stage. You keep them, so you see the progress rather than covering a slot.
